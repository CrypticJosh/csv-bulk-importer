CSV Bulk Importer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

CSV Bulk Importer is a Google Sheets add-on designed to help users import and combine CSV files into Google Sheets.

Information We Access

When you use CSV Bulk Importer, the add-on may access the Google spreadsheet you are currently using in order to insert imported CSV data into the selected sheet tab.

The add-on does not request access to unrelated spreadsheets.

CSV Files

CSV files are selected by you from your computer and processed for the purpose of importing their contents into your selected Google Sheets destination.

CSV files are not uploaded to a separate server operated by the developer. The add-on does not maintain a database of your CSV files.

Google Account Information

Google may provide basic account information, such as your email address, as part of the Google authorization process. CSV Bulk Importer does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your spreadsheet data, CSV data, or personal information to third parties.

We do not use your spreadsheet or CSV data for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

Data Retention

CSV Bulk Importer does not maintain a separate database containing your imported CSV data. Data inserted into your Google Sheet remains subject to Google's storage and privacy policies.

Security

Access to Google Sheets is controlled through Google's authorization system. You can revoke the add-on's authorization through your Google Account settings.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or CSV Bulk Importer, please contact the developer using the support information provided on the application's support page.
